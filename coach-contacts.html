Common questions.

How are the coach contacts verified?
Our database is manually verified and updated every quarter. We cross-reference university athletic department websites and NCAA/NAIA directories to ensure accuracy.
What information is included for each coach?
Every list includes the coach's name, university and email address. The NAIA and NJCAA lists also include each coach's title and phone number, and the NCAA Division II list includes phone numbers.
How do I receive the database after purchase?
Straight after payment, your list is emailed to you as a PDF attachment. It usually arrives within a few minutes. If you can't see it, check your spam folder or contact us and we'll resend it.
Can I use this alongside The Next Level's recruitment packages?
Absolutely — many athletes purchase the database to do their own outreach in parallel with our managed recruitment service for maximum exposure.
